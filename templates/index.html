The Nwachukwu Model blends the Bayesian-regression estimator of Shah & Zhang (2014), "Bayesian Regression and Bitcoin", with the Combinatorial Fusion Analysis (CFA) framework of Wu, Ye, Xu & Hsu, "Bitcoin Price Prediction using Machine Learning and Combinatorial Fusion Analysis" (IEEE CAI). Five diverse base models (Bayesian, momentum, mean-reversion, EMA/MACD, random-walk) each become a scoring system; they are fused by score combination weighted by cognitive-diversity strength, applied live to recent 15-minute and 1-hour candles.
